# Disclosure timeline

- **Discovery.** The findings in this repository were developed through non-destructive technical
  research on production Dominican digital cédulas, examined with the
  express permission of their holders, issued between early 2026 and September
  2026, using only documented ICAO/ISO protocols (PACE, Passive/Chip Authentication, standard
  file reads) plus negative and boundary tests that do not modify the card's persistent state.
- **Disclosure.** The findings were reported to the issuing authority, the Junta Central
  Electoral (JCE), before any publication.
- **Publication.** This repository is the public, post-disclosure publication. Its contents were reviewed before publication to exclude citizen data, credentials,
  and other material excluded by this project's evidence-handling rule (see
  [`README_EN.md`](README_EN.md)).
